# Touch controls

The default is **Joystick & aim crosshair**. Move with the stick on the left, drag to look, and use the action buttons on the right. Open **Options → Touch Controls** for sensitivity, inversion, deadzone, stick position, size, opacity, handedness, and two other schemes:

- **Joystick & tap to interact**
- **D-pad & tap to interact**

With either tap scheme, drag to look, tap a block to use or place, tap an entity to attack, and hold a finger still to mine. The Use button works with held items such as food or bows. Tap Sprint or Crouch once to enable it and again to disable it. Jump and crouch also control swimming and flight. Double-tap Jump to toggle Creative flight when available.

Use **Pause → Customize Controls** to move controls over the world preview. Select a control to change its size or opacity. **Reset** restores that item, **Reset All** restores the current layout, and **Save** applies changes. **Cancel** discards the draft. Layouts are shared across instances.

Local worlds use analogue movement. Remote Java servers use digital movement. Third-person view can shift the apparent target because interaction still starts at the player’s eyes.
